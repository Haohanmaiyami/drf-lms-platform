Delete Your LingLoop Account

LingLoop users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the LingLoop app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

If you cannot access the app

Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your LingLoop account.

Email: ayarsen1995@gmail.com

Include “Account deletion request” in the subject line. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account and profile information
  • Email address
  • Learning progress
  • Speaking attempts
  • Uploaded audio recordings
  • Transcripts
  • AI-generated feedback
  • Personal settings

Data retention

Some information may remain in secure backups for up to 30 days after deletion. Certain records may be retained longer when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance with legal obligations.

After the applicable retention period, the data is permanently deleted unless a longer period is required by law.
